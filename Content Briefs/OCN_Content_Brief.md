| **CPHON** | ONCC pediatric hematology/oncology nursing (peds, not adult) | Comparison row |

| **ONS chemotherapy/immunotherapy certificate** | A continuing-education certificate course from ONS, not a board certification | One line in overview disambiguation |

OCN is ONCC's foundational adult-oncology RN board certification. The most common confusions are with the ONS chemotherapy/immunotherapy certificate (a course, not a credential) and with the narrower ONCC specialty credentials.

## Content Outline (Headings + Writer Guidance)

### H1: OCN Certification: Eligibility, Exam Blueprint, Fees & Renewal

### Lede (no heading, opens above-the-fold)
- One paragraph (~45 words). Answer-first: OCN (Oncology Certified Nurse) is the foundational board certification for adult oncology nursing, issued by the Oncology Nursing Certification Corporation (ONCC). It validates specialty knowledge across the cancer continuum - from screening through symptom management and end-of-life care.

### Hero pills
- Questions: 165 items (facts.question_count_total)
- Duration: 3 hours (facts.time_limit_hours)
- Renewal: 4 years (facts.renewal_years)
- Pass rate: ~62% (pass_rate_history[0], medium confidence)

### H2: OCN certification at a glance (overview)
- Name OCN and ONCC in the first sentence. State it is ONCC's foundational adult-oncology RN credential and an NCCA-accredited program.
- Note it is a board certification (exam), not a course. Disambiguate from the ONS chemotherapy/immunotherapy certificate course.
- Stats grid: 165 items (145 scored, 20 pretest); 3 hours; ~62% 2024 pass rate (medium confidence); 4-year renewal.
- Fact box (info): the 2026 Test Content Outline is unchanged from 2025; six subject areas; only generic drug names appear on the test; same-day results.

### H2: Am I eligible for the OCN exam? (eligibility)
- Answer-first: eligibility is gated, not recommended. All four criteria must be met at application AND examination.
- List: active unencumbered RN license (US/territories/Canada); 2,000 hours adult oncology nursing practice in prior 48 months; 24 months RN experience in prior 48 months; 10 oncology CE contact hours in prior 36 months.
- "You are a strong candidate if..." list anchored on the four gates plus practical signals (comfortable with chemo administration, symptom management, oncologic emergencies).

### H2: OCN exam blueprint - six subject areas (blueprint)
- Answer-first: six domains, weighted by percentage, summing to 100%.
- Domains (official 2026 ONCC outline): Symptom Management and Supportive Care 25%, Treatment Modalities 20%, Oncologic Emergencies 16%, Oncology Nursing Practice 15%, Care Continuum 14%, Psychosocial Dimensions of Care 10%.
- Guidance: Symptom Management + Treatment Modalities = 45% of the exam; allocate study time accordingly. Don't neglect Psychosocial (10%) - it is the most under-studied.

### H2: Cost, scheduling, and renewal (cost)
- Fee rows: ONS/APHON member $300; non-member $420; DoubleTake add-on +$100 (selected at registration). Note Oncology Nursing Month 20% discount promos appear periodically.
- Scheduling: apply via ONCC; ATT arrives within ~4-6 weeks and opens an almost-immediate 90-day testing window; computer-based via PSI; same-day results.
- Renewal subsection: valid 4 years; Option 1 (Practice Hours + ILNA points) is most popular; 1,000 oncology practice hours in prior 30 months + 12 months RN experience; ~100 ILNA points matched to the current blueprint (medium confidence); Options 2 & 3 use re-testing.

### H2: How hard is the OCN exam? (passrate)
- Answer-first: recent OCN pass rates run about 62% overall (first-time ~65%), medium confidence; verify against ONCC's annual statistics.
- Passing score is a scaled score of at least 55, set by a criterion-referenced Passing Score Study - not a simple percent correct.
- Lever: structured study + full-length timed practice; the 2025 Passing Score Task Force reviewed the cut score.

### H2: A 10-week OCN study plan (plan)
- ~80-120 study hours across the six domains, ramping practice-question volume, peaking with a full-length timed exam.

### H2: Sample OCN question (sample)

- 6-8 high-yield terms mapped to domains: tumor lysis syndrome, neutropenic fever/sepsis, SIADH, extravasation/vesicant, CRS (cytokine release syndrome), nadir, performance status (ECOG), spinal cord compression.
