## 2. Critical Disambiguation (Read First)

**CWCN is awarded by the Wound, Ostomy and Continence Nursing Certification Board (WOCNCB)** — the credentialing arm separate from the WOCN Society (the membership and education body). The exam is administered through **PSI testing centers** (formerly Applied Measurement Professionals). Writer must name WOCNCB as the awarding body in section 1 and reinforce it whenever fees, eligibility, and renewal appear. (`facts.awarding_body`, `facts.exam_format`)

**Credentials readers confuse with CWCN:**

| Credential / Concept | Awarding body | Why it confuses people | How to handle |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CWOCN** | WOCNCB | One-letter difference; covers all three WOC specialties | One-line contrast: CWOCN (Certified Wound, Ostomy, and Continence Nurse) covers wound, ostomy, and continence in one credential. CWCN is wound-only. (`facts.common_confusions`) |
| **CWON** | WOCNCB | Two-specialty bundle; appears in WOCNCB's menu | One-line contrast: CWON covers wound and ostomy; no continence component. |
| **WCC** | NAWCO | Often Googled by candidates without a BSN | One-line contrast: WCC (Wound Care Certified) is issued by the NAWCO and accepts RNs, LPNs, PTs, MDs, and other clinicians. CWCN is WOCNCB-only and requires a BSN. |
| **CWS** | American Board of Wound Management (ABWM) | Interdisciplinary credential; appears in employer postings | One-line contrast: CWS (Certified Wound Specialist) is open to any healthcare professional with a bachelor's degree (RN, PT, MD, etc.). CWCN is RN-only. |
| **CWCA** | NAWCO | Sounds like a CWCN tier | One-line contrast: CWCA (Wound Care Certified Associate) is an entry-level NAWCO credential for non-licensed staff. Not interchangeable with CWCN. |

Writer must spell **Certified Wound Care Nurse** out fully on first mention, then use "CWCN" throughout. Never say "CWCN course" — CWCN is an exam-based board credential, not a course.

### H2 — Am I Eligible for the CWCN Exam?

- Open with a table of eligibility requirements pulled from the WOCNCB credential page. (`facts.eligibility`)

| Requirement | Current Rule |
|---|---|
| License | Active, unrestricted RN license in the US or its territories |
| Education | **Baccalaureate (BSN) or higher degree in nursing — a hard gate** |
| Pathway A — Traditional | Graduation from an accredited WOC Nursing Education Program (**WOCNEP**) within the past 5 years |
| Pathway B — Experiential | **50 wound care CE hours** in the past 5 years AND **1,500 direct patient-care hours** in wound care in the past 5 years, **with at least 375 of those hours in the 12 months immediately preceding application** |

- Important nuance: unlike some wound credentials (WCC, CWS) that accept LPNs, PTs, or non-BSN RNs, **WOCNCB enforces the BSN requirement** as a hard gate. ASN/diploma RNs must complete BSN coursework before applying — there is no waiver.
- Add a short Q&A block:
  - *"Do home health wound visits count toward the 1,500-hour experiential pathway?"* — Yes. Direct patient-care hours in wound assessment, treatment, and education count regardless of setting.
  - *"What if I graduated from a WOCNEP six years ago?"* — The 5-year window applies. After 5 years, candidates default to the experiential pathway.
  - *"I'm a Canadian RN — can I sit CWCN?"* — WOCNCB credentials are US-focused; international applicants should contact WOCNCB to confirm eligibility before applying. (`facts.eligibility`)

### H2 — How Do I Apply for the CWCN Exam?

Numbered step-by-step:

1. Confirm BSN status and select the pathway (Traditional WOCNEP or Experiential 50 CE + 1,500 hours).
2. Gather documentation — RN license verification, transcripts, CE certificates, and a log of direct wound care hours signed by a supervisor.
3. Complete the online exam application on the WOCNCB site and pay the **$395 single-specialty fee** (or one of the multi-specialty bundle rates if you are sitting CWCN with CCCN, COCN, or CFCN simultaneously). (`facts.fee`)
4. WOCNCB confirms eligibility and PSI sends the Authorization to Test (ATT) — you now have a **6-month window** to schedule and complete the exam.
5. Schedule your seat with PSI online (PSI center).
6. Test day: bring valid government-issued photo ID matching the application name.
7. Score reporting: candidates receive an unofficial pass/fail result at the test center; official results from WOCNCB arrive by email shortly after.

### H2 — How Do I Renew CWCN Certification?

- CWCN is valid for **5 years**. (`facts.renewal`)
- Recertification offers **two paths**: (1) re-take the CWCN exam, or (2) complete the **Professional Growth Program (PGP)** — an online portfolio totaling **80 PGP points**, of which at least **10 must be continuing education hours in wound care**. (`facts.renewal`)
- The PGP recognizes a wide range of professional activities — continuing education, professional society memberships, publishing, precepting, committee service, and presentations — which gives clinically active CWCNs flexibility to recertify without sitting the exam again.
- WOCNCB charges no annual maintenance fees during the 5-year cycle.
- Lapsed credential: holders who let CWCN expire must sit the current exam again to reinstate.

### H2 — CWCN vs CWOCN, CWON, WCC: Which One Fits?

| Credential | For | Awarding body | One-line scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CWCN** | BSN-prepared RNs | WOCNCB | Wound care only |
| **CWOCN** | BSN-prepared RNs | WOCNCB | Wound + ostomy + continence — all three specialties |
| **CWON** | BSN-prepared RNs | WOCNCB | Wound + ostomy (no continence) |
| **WCC** | RNs, LPNs, PTs, MDs, others | NAWCO | Wound care — interdisciplinary, broader eligibility |
| **CWS** | Any clinician with bachelor's degree | ABWM | Wound care — interdisciplinary, advanced practice tier |

(`facts.common_confusions`)

**Take CWCN if you are a BSN-prepared RN focused on wound care and not planning to take on ostomy or continence patients.** Pick CWOCN if your role spans all three specialties; CWON if you handle wound and ostomy. Consider WCC or CWS if you do not hold a BSN or are a non-RN clinician.
