## 2. Critical Disambiguation

- **CEN vs. TCRN** — both BCEN. CEN = breadth of ED practice; TCRN = trauma across the full continuum. (see facts.common_confusions)
- **CEN vs. CPEN** — CPEN is BCEN's pediatric emergency certification; CEN covers all ages but is adult-weighted.
- **CEN vs. CFRN/CTRN** — flight/ground transport credentials, not bedside ED.
- **CEN vs. TNCC / ENPC** — TNCC and ENPC are ENA *provider courses*, not board certifications. Only CEN appears as credentials after the RN line. This is the most common candidate confusion to clear early.
