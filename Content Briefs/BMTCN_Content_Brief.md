### H2: Am I eligible for the BMTCN/TCTCN exam? (id=eligibility)
Two prose paragraphs covering the verbatim eligibility from research:
- Para 1: Active unencumbered RN license (US, territories, Canada); minimum 2 years (24 months) RN experience within last 4 years; 2,000 hours of HSCT/cellular-therapy nursing practice within last 4 years; 10 contact hours of CE in HSCT/cellular therapy (or oncology academic elective) within last 3 years. Up to 5 of 10 CE hours may be CME/CPE.
- Para 2: Practice may be direct or indirect patient care, including clinical practice, administration, education, research, or consultation - position must require RN/APRN. TCTCN wording shift updates "blood and marrow stem cell transplantation" to "hematopoietic stem cell transplantation or cellular therapy nursing."
- Candidate list: 4-5 bullets describing strong-candidate profiles (allo/auto BMT inpatient nurses, CAR-T administration teams, pediatric HSCT, ambulatory BMT clinic, BMT survivorship/long-term follow-up).

### H2: BMTCN exam blueprint - six domains (id=blueprint)
Two prose paragraphs:
- Para 1: Note that the 2025 BMTCN blueprint (effective through the September 1, 2025 application deadline) has six domains: Foundations of Transplant 19%, Transplant Process and Infusion 17%, Early Post-Transplant Management 21%, Late Post-Transplant Management 20%, Quality of Life 11%, Professional Performance 12%. Early + Late Post-Transplant Management together carry 41% — the heaviest study weight.
- Para 2: Mention the 2026 TCTCN blueprint shift (effective January 2026): the credential now explicitly separates HSCT and cellular-therapy management into parallel domains, increases the Foundations weight to 22%, and reduces Quality of Life (to 8%) and Professional Performance (to 4%) to make room. Candidates applying in 2026 should study against the TCTCN blueprint, not the legacy BMTCN one.
- Domains array uses the 2025 BMTCN weights (since that is what the BMTCN-branded exam tested) with a note.
