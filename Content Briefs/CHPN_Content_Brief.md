## 2. Critical Disambiguation (Read First)

**CHPN is awarded by the Hospice and Palliative Credentialing Center (HPCC)** — the credentialing arm of the Hospice and Palliative Nurses Association (HPNA). It is administered by **PSI Exams** at PSI test centers and via Live Remote Proctoring (see `facts.awarding_body` and `facts.exam_vendor` in research.json). HPNA is the membership/professional society. HPCC is the certification body. Writer must name HPCC as the awarding body in section 1 and reinforce it whenever fees, eligibility, and renewal appear.

**Credentials readers confuse with CHPN:**

| Credential / Concept | Awarding body | Why it confuses people | How to handle |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ACHPN** | HPCC | Sounds like a tier of CHPN; many CHPN holders Google ACHPN by mistake | One-line contrast: ACHPN is the advanced-practice (NP/CNS) version of CHPN. CHPN is RN-level. (`facts.common_confusions`) |
| **CHPPN** | HPCC | Adds one letter; pediatric specialty | One-line contrast: CHPPN is for pediatric RNs in hospice/palliative care. CHPN is the adult-focused RN credential (covers adult-lifespan patients). |
| **CHPLN** | HPCC | Similar prefix; targets LPN/LVN | One-line contrast: CHPLN is HPCC's LPN/LVN credential. CHPN is RN-only. |
| **CHPNA** | HPCC | Sometimes Googled by RNs in error | One-line contrast: CHPNA is HPCC's nursing-assistant certification. Different scope and eligibility. |
| **OCN** (Oncology Certified Nurse) | ONCC | Overlapping symptom-management content | One-line contrast: OCN is for oncology RNs across the full cancer continuum. CHPN focuses on end-of-life and serious-illness care across all diagnoses, not just oncology. |
| **CHPCA** | HPCC | "C" prefix; appears in the HPCC menu | One-line note: CHPCA is for hospice/palliative administrators. Not a clinical RN credential and no longer available by exam since 2018. |

Writer must spell **Certified Hospice and Palliative Nurse** out fully on first mention, then use "CHPN" throughout. Never say "CHPN course" — CHPN is an exam-based board credential, not a course.

### H2 — How Do I Apply for the CHPN Exam?

Numbered step-by-step:

1. Confirm 500/1,000-hour eligibility against your timesheets.
2. Decide on HPNA membership before applying — the HPNA-member fee is **$305**, non-member is **$445**, so the ~$140 fee delta usually outweighs the cost of joining HPNA. (`facts.fee_member`, `facts.fee_nonmember`)
3. Choose your testing window and confirm the application deadline (Feb 15 / May 15 / Aug 15 / Nov 15).
4. Complete the online application on the HPCC credential page. Pay with Visa, MasterCard, Discover, or American Express.
5. PSI confirms eligibility within several business days.
6. Choose between PSI in-person testing or Live Remote Proctoring and schedule your seat.
7. Test day: bring valid government-issued photo ID that matches the application name. For LRP: complete the system check ahead of time and clear the testing room per PSI requirements.
8. Score reporting: in-person candidates receive scores at the test center before leaving; LRP candidates receive scores within 24 hours.

### H2 — How Hard Is the CHPN Exam? (Pass Rate)

- HPCC publishes year-by-year pass rates. (`pass_rate_history`)
- **2025**: 1,190 first-time candidates, 825 passed = **69.3% first-time pass rate**. Total (first-time plus repeaters) = 907 of 1,358 = **66.8%**. Repeater pass rate was 48.8%.
- **2024**: 1,288 first-time candidates, 939 passed = **72.9% first-time pass rate**. Total = 990 of 1,386 = **71.4%**.
- Writer guidance: be honest about the year-over-year dip (72.9% → 69.3% first-time). Possible drivers include the May 2026 release of the 2027 Detailed Content Outline introducing content shifts. Frame as: *"first-time pass sits in the high 60s to low 70s — meaningfully higher than TCRN (59%) but lower than the 80%+ rates seen on some narrower exams."*
- Footnote that the repeater pass rate of 48.8% means a casual second attempt without structural changes to study habits is risky — this is the argument for reTEST Assured plus an actual restudy plan, not just rebooking.

### H2 — How Do I Renew CHPN Certification?

- CHPN is valid for **4 years**. (`facts.renewal_cycle_years`)
- Recertification runs through **HPAR — Hospice and Palliative Accrual for Recertification** — an online portfolio system (LearningBuilder) where holders accumulate **100 HPAR points** over the cycle. **1 contact hour of CE = 1 HPAR point.** Other professional activities (publishing, precepting, committee service) also count. (`facts.renewal_program`)
- New since the original CHPN renewal model: a **Situational Judgment Exercise (SJE)** is now part of recertification — case-based scenarios that test clinical decision-making beyond the initial-exam content. (`facts.renewal_program`)
- Clinical-practice hours requirement during the cycle exists but is not numerically specified on the public HPAR landing page — confirm the current minimum via the candidate's HPAR Packet at the time of renewal. (`gaps`)
- Lapsed credential: holders who let CHPN expire must sit the current exam again to reinstate. There is no extended grace period.

### H2 — CHPN vs ACHPN, CHPPN, CHPLN: Which One Fits?

| Credential | For | Awarding body | One-line scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CHPN** | RNs | HPCC | RN-level hospice and palliative nursing for adult patients |
| **ACHPN** | APRNs (NP, CNS) | HPCC | Advanced-practice hospice/palliative — prescribing, advanced symptom management, ICF-level care planning |
| **CHPPN** | Pediatric RNs | HPCC | Hospice/palliative nursing for pediatric patients |
| **CHPLN** | LPNs / LVNs | HPCC | Hospice/palliative practice at the LPN/LVN scope |

(`facts.common_confusions`)

**Take CHPN if you are an RN spending most of your time in hospice or palliative care for adults.** Move to ACHPN once you complete an APRN program. CHPPN is the parallel credential if your patients are pediatric. CHPLN is for LPN/LVN colleagues.
