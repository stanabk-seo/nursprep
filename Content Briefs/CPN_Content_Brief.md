## 2. Critical Disambiguation (Read First)

**CPN is awarded by the Pediatric Nursing Certification Board (PNCB)** and administered by **Prometric** testing centers (see `facts.awarding_body` and `facts.exam_format` in research.json). Writer must name PNCB as the awarding body in section 1 and reinforce it wherever fees, eligibility, and renewal appear. Never call CPN a "course" — it is an exam-based board credential.

**Credentials readers confuse with CPN:**

| Credential / Concept | Awarding body | Why it confuses people | How to handle |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CPNP-PC / CPNP-AC** | PNCB | Same issuing body; "pediatric nurse" in the name | One-line contrast: these are advanced-practice (APRN, graduate-degree) Pediatric Nurse Practitioner credentials. CPN is the RN-level generalist credential. (`facts.common_confusions`) |
| **CPEN** | BCEN | Both are RN-level pediatric credentials | One-line contrast: CPEN (Certified Pediatric Emergency Nurse) is emergency-specific from BCEN. CPN covers pediatric care broadly across inpatient and outpatient settings. |
| **PMHS** | PNCB | Also a PNCB pediatric specialty | One-line contrast: PMHS is the Pediatric Primary Care Mental Health Specialist add-on, not the generalist CPN. |

Spell out **Certified Pediatric Nurse** fully on first mention, then use "CPN" throughout.

## 5. Content Outline (Headings + Writer Guidance)

### H1 — CPN Certification: A Complete Guide for Pediatric Nurses

**Intro (140–200 words):**
- Sentence 1: one-line answer to *What is the CPN exam?*
- Name the awarding body (**PNCB — Pediatric Nursing Certification Board**) and scope (RN-level pediatric nursing across inpatient and outpatient settings). (`facts.awarding_body`)
- Disambiguate: CPN is a board credential, not a course; computer-based at Prometric. (`facts.exam_format`)
- Preview sections; place "CPN" in sentence 1 and once more in the last intro line.

### H2 — What is the CPN exam? (Overview)
- Full credential name **Certified Pediatric Nurse**; awarding body **PNCB**; launched **1989**. (`facts.year_launched`)
- Credibility data point: **more than 30,000 nurses actively hold CPN**. (`facts.active_holders`)
- Definition block for "pediatric nursing experience" — direct care AND indirect roles (teaching, administration, research, consultation) all count. (`facts.eligibility`)

### H2 — Am I eligible for the CPN exam?
- Answer-first: yes, with an active unrestricted RN license (US, Canada, or US territory) and one of two experience pathways. (`facts.eligibility`)
- "You're a strong candidate if…" checklist built from the two pathways: 1,800 pediatric hours in the past 24 months, OR five years pediatric RN experience with 3,000 hours in five years (≥1,000 in the past 24 months).

### H2 — CPN exam blueprint — four domains
- Four domains with weights summing to 100%: Health Promotion 23%, Assessment 35%, Planning and Management 33%, Professional Responsibilities 9%. (`blueprint`)
- Note cross-cutting threads PNCB names: safety, growth and development, evidence-based practice. (`blueprint_note`)
- Guidance: Assessment + Planning and Management are two-thirds of the exam — weight study time there.

### H2 — Cost, scheduling, and scoring
- $300 initial fee (includes $100 non-refundable registration); $245 retake. (`facts.fee`)
- Computer-based at Prometric; 175 items (150 scored + 25 pretest), 3-hour limit. (`facts.exam_format`)
- 90-day scheduling window after approval. (`facts.scheduling`)
- Scoring: scaled 200–800, passing cut point = 400. (`facts.passing_score`)
- Renewal subsection: **annual** recertification, Nov 1–Jan 31, **15 contact hours** per year (clinical-practice and CE equivalents accepted). (`facts.renewal`)

### H2 — How hard is the CPN exam? (Pass rate)
- Lead with the verified figure: **~76% pass rate (2022)**. (`pass_rate_history`)
- Be transparent that PNCB's most recent year-specific CPN figure was not verifiable for this draft; do not state a 2024 number. (research gap, see §7)

### H2 — A study plan for the CPN exam
- 8–10 week plan, ~60–90 study hours, ramped practice questions, one full-length timed run.

### H2 — Sample CPN-style question
- One pediatric scenario item with four options and a rationale. Mark clearly as illustrative, not a released PNCB item.

### H2 — Key CPN terms every candidate should know
- 6–8 pediatric definitions (e.g., growth-and-development milestones, family-centered care, pediatric early warning score, weight-based dosing).

### H2 — CPN vs. CPEN, CPNP, PMHS
- Comparison table per `facts.common_confusions`.

### H2 — Frequently asked questions
- 8–10 real candidate questions (what CPN stands for, eligibility, cost, retake, renewal, difficulty, recognition).

### H2 — Trusted sources
- 6–10 entries from `sources_index`.
